1.3.3 (21 Nov 2026)
- Windows package (tools/package-windows.sh): without the libraries PianoBooster does not
  use: Qt6Network and Qt6Xml, the Qt plugins for touch input, network information and
  TLS, and the GLib libraries they needed (11 DLLs fewer).

1.3.2 (21 Nov 2026)
- The settings of each song (hand, speed and the MIDI channel of each hand) and the last
  song of each folder are kept in the library database, instead of a "pb.cfg" file in
  each folder. Copies of the same song in different folders share their settings.
  - The first time a folder with a "pb.cfg" file is opened, its settings are moved to the
    database and the file is deleted. Settings already in the database are kept.
- Piano Booster no longer needs the Qt Xml module.

1.3.1 (20 Nov 2026)
- Section editor: choose the instrument to practise in the new "Instrument" list, with
  the same items as the list of instruments of the practice window (MIDI channels). It
  starts with the one chosen there.
  - Its notes are shown in colour, by hand, as they are practised: if it is one of the
    two hands of the piano part, both hands; otherwise only that channel, with the hands
    told apart by track or at middle C. The other instruments are grey, and hidden when
    "Other instruments" is unticked. Before, the piano part was always the one shown.
  - The drums are shown if they are the instrument chosen.
  - The piano roll shows the range of the notes that are shown.
  - "Practise" also chooses that instrument in the practice window.

1.3.0 (20 Nov 2026)
- Section editor (Song / Section Editor..., Ctrl+E, or "Editor..." under the section list
  of the side panel): a window to create and change the sections of a song on a piano
  roll of the whole song, without having to play it to reach their start and end.
  - The piano roll scrolls with the mouse wheel and zooms with Ctrl + wheel or the zoom
    buttons; an overview of the whole song above it shows where you are, and a click on
    it goes to that place. The notes of the right hand are blue, those of the left hand
    green and those of the other instruments grey (they can be hidden).
  - Drag on the ruler or on the notes to select a stretch, and drag its edges to change
    it. The edges snap to bars, beats, half or quarter beats, or nothing. The start and
    the end can also be typed as bar and beat; the end is the place just before which
    the section stops.
  - The saved sections are shown on a strip under the ruler and in a list: choose one to
    see and change it, then "Save"; "New..." saves the selection as a new section.
  - "Play" listens to the selection (or to the song from the place shown), at a speed of
    its own and repeating if wanted, through the MIDI output, with a red line showing
    where it is playing. Space starts and stops it.
  - "Practise" closes the editor and practises the selection or the section, repeating.
  - Sections created in the practice window keep working as before: both windows use
    the same sections.

1.2.1 (18 Nov 2026)
- Songs whose time signature changes (e.g. 4/4 at the start and 12/8 later): the start
  and end of a section, typed in "Start / Repeat" or saved as a named section, are now
  worked out with the time signature of their own bar, not with the one being played.
  Before, typing "bar 21, beat 7" in a 12/8 bar while the song was at its 4/4 start was
  taken as "bar 22, beat 3".
  - The time signatures of the whole song are read when it is loaded, and the bars are
    counted with them.
  - The beat fields only go up to the last beat of the bar typed (e.g. 4 or 12).
  - Sections saved before in such songs may be in the wrong place: set them again and
    press "Update".

1.2.0 (17 Nov 2026)
- Named sections of a song, to practise them on their own (e.g. "Intro", "Bars 12-16"):
  - Choose one in the new "Section" list of the side panel: it is played from its start
    to its end, repeating. "Whole song" goes back to playing all of it.
  - "+" saves the current start and end (set in "Start / Repeat") as a new section; the
    other buttons update the selected section with the current start and end, rename it
    or delete it.
  - Sections are kept in the library database and belong to the song, so they are kept
    if the song is moved or renamed. The library shows how many sections each song has.
  - They are called sections, not parts, because "Parts" already means the tracks
    (instruments) of the MIDI file.
- The side panel scrolls when it is taller than the screen, instead of making the window
  taller than the screen (which kept it from being maximized properly).
- The summary on the "Start / Repeat" button is shortened when it is too long.

1.1.1 (17 Nov 2026)
- The window opens maximized. Its size when it is not maximized is still saved,
  and used when it is restored down.
- The start bar and beat moved from the top bar to the popup of the repeated section,
  now called "Start and Repeat", next to the end bar and beat. The button that opens it
  shows a summary (e.g. "bar 12 → bar 14 ↻"). The top bar was wider than many screens,
  so the window could not be restored down to a smaller size.
- The popup stays on the screen when its button is near the right side.

1.1.0 (17 Nov 2026)
- New song library (File/Library..., Ctrl+B, or the Library button in the side panel):
  - Favourite songs, recently opened songs and the MIDI files in your own folders
    ("locations"), with a search box. The Booster Music course is the first location
    when it has been unzipped (on Windows, unzip BoosterMusicBooks.zip and add it).
  - Shows the duration, number of bars and time signature of each song, when it was
    last opened and how many times.
  - Kept in a database (library.db) next to the settings. Songs are recognised by their
    contents, so a moved or renamed song keeps its data.
- The side panel shows the current song (name, folder, time signature, key, tempo,
  duration and number of bars) instead of the book and song lists.
- The window title keeps the version when a song is loaded, and the program name is no
  longer translated in it.
- Building now needs the Qt SQL module with its SQLite driver.

1.0.5 (17 Nov 2026)
- The bar numbers are shown in the score, above the start of each bar.
- Bars are numbered from 1, like in printed music, everywhere: the bar counter,
  the start bar and the end bar of the repeated section.

1.0.4 (17 Nov 2026)
- Fixed the bar lines and beat markers in the score when the time signature changes
  (e.g. from 4/4 to 12/8): the new time signature was used from one beat too early and
  the beat count was not restarted, so the bar lines were drawn in the middle of bars.

1.0.3 (17 Nov 2026)
- Fixed the start position not starting on the intended note:
  - The start is now set as a bar and a beat (with decimals, e.g. 2.5), and stored
    as an exact position instead of a bar number rounded to one decimal.
  - "Save this Bar Number" (the flag) now stores the exact position of the note being
    waited for, so playing starts on that note.
  - The bar position was a little ahead of the notes when waiting for the pianist.
  - The decimals of a bar position were wrong for time signatures other than 4/4.
- The end of the repeated section is also an exact end bar and beat (instead of a number
  of bars after the start), and includes the notes at that position. It can be set to the
  current notes with a flag button, like the start.
